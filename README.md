## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crownhold**. This trainer reduces the waiting involved in Crownhold's resource, construction, research, and army systems. It provides economy boosts, instant building and research, expanded army capacity, progression unlocks, camera controls, and other quality-of-life options for offline play.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to test kingdom strategies, speed up resource management, experiment with army sizes, explore the prestige system more quickly, or reduce repetitive waiting during offline play.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Gold** — Prevents gold shortages and provides the funds needed for construction, upgrades, exploration, and military actions.
- **Gold Multiplier** — Multiplies gold gains with an adjustable value from 1x to 50x.
- **Instant Building** — Completes castle and realm construction immediately.
- **Maximum Resources** — Refills the game's available resource pools for faster kingdom development.
- **Instant Research** — Completes research actions without waiting for their normal duration.
- **Unlock Everything** — Unlocks available progression content and strategic options in the save.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts supported movement and navigation speed from 1x to 5x.
- **Free Camera** — Enables unrestricted camera control for viewing the kingdom and surrounding areas.
- **Hide HUD** — Toggles the interface off for unobstructed views of the game world.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Ignore Unit Cap** — Removes normal army-size restrictions so larger forces can be assembled.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Crownhold is primarily controlled through its kingdom and world-management interface rather than direct character movement. Use the trainer's movement-speed control where supported, and enable Free Camera when you need a broader view of the realm.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage gold and other kingdom resources through the game's resource and development screens. Maximum Resources can refill available supplies, while Unlimited Gold or the Gold Multiplier can support construction, research, exploration, and military spending.

### World & Environment

Expand the starting castle, discover foreign lands, establish relationships with trading partners and other factions, and grow the kingdom beyond its original borders. Instant Building and Instant Research accelerate expansion, while Unlock Everything grants immediate access to available progression content.

### Combat & Weapons

Raise armies and use them to lay siege to enemy territories. Ignore Unit Cap allows forces larger than the normal limit, while Unlimited Gold and Maximum Resources help fund military preparation. Use combat-related options carefully because extreme values can disrupt normal progression and balance.
